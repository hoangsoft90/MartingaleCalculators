📊 Grid Survival Simulator — The #1 offline risk analysis tool for Grid & Martingale traders.

Stop guessing. Start calculating. Before you risk real money on a Grid strategy, let this app show you exactly how many levels your account can survive.

🎯 WHO IS THIS FOR?
• Forex & Gold traders using Grid or Martingale strategies
• MT4/MT5 traders who want to stress-test before going live
• Anyone who wants to understand their real risk exposure

💡 KEY FEATURES

🔢 Quick Calculator
→ Input your account balance, leverage, lot size, multiplier & grid distance
→ Supports Buy & Sell directions
→ Advanced settings: Spread, Commission, Swap, Holding Days

📊 Risk Dashboard
→ Survivable Levels: How many grid levels can your account handle?
→ Max Drawdown %: Worst-case scenario analysis
→ Stop-out Price: When does your account blow up?
→ Basket Breakeven: Where does your grid recover?
→ Constraint Check: PASS/FAIL on your custom risk limits

📈 Price Ladder (Interactive Chart)
→ Visual grid levels on an interactive line chart
→ Tap any level to see detailed margin & exposure
→ Average entry, breakeven & stop-out lines displayed

🎚️ What-If Slider
→ Drag to simulate price movements (±10%)
→ See triggered levels, drawdown & margin level in real-time
→ Instant "what happens if price drops 500 pips?"

🔄 Reverse Mode
→ Set your max drawdown, margin level & loss limits
→ App calculates the MAXIMUM initial lot you can use
→ Find your bottleneck constraint instantly

💾 Save & Load Strategies
→ Save up to 5 strategies offline
→ Load & compare different configurations
→ All data stored locally — 100% private

📤 Share as Image
→ Generate a clean summary card
→ Share via any messaging app (WhatsApp, Telegram, Zalo...)

⚡ WHY TRADERS LOVE IT
• 100% OFFLINE — No internet required, no data sent anywhere
• INSTANT RESULTS — Pure Dart engine, no server lag
• PRECISE CALCULATIONS — 37+ unit tests ensuring accuracy
• NO LOGIN NEEDED — Open and use immediately
• LIGHTWEIGHT — Fast install, minimal battery usage

🛡️ TRUST & TRANSPARENCY
• Open-source engine (packages/grid_engine)
• Every calculation includes assumptions panel
• "Not financial advice" disclaimer on all outputs
• Sentry error tracking for crash-free experience

📱 SUPPORTED INSTRUMENTS
• XAUUSD (Gold)
• EURUSD
• More symbols coming soon!

⚠️ DISCLAIMER
This app is for educational and analytical purposes only. It does not provide financial advice. All trading decisions are your own responsibility. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

🌐 OFFLINE FIRST
Your data never leaves your device. No accounts. No cloud. No tracking. Your strategies are 100% private.

Made with ❤️ by traders, for traders.

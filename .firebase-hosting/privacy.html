Privacy Policy

Grid Survival Simulator · Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Summary: Grid Survival Simulator is an offline-only app. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Your strategies and calculations never leave your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Grid Survival Simulator is designed as a fully offline application. We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Financial information (account numbers, balances, trading history)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavior tracking
  • Crash reports containing personal data

2. How the App Works

All calculations, saved strategies, and user inputs are processed entirely on your device. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party. When you save a strategy, it is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (Hive).

3. Advertising

This app displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information for ad serving purposes, including:

  • Advertising ID (can be reset or limited in device settings)
  • Device type and OS version
  • General geographic location (country/region level only)

This data collection is handled entirely by Google AdMob and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to or control over the data collected by AdMob.

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated in this app is:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. Privacy Policy

No other analytics, tracking, or data collection SDKs are used.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breaches on our end. All locally stored data (saved strategies) remains on your device and is cleared when you uninstall the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

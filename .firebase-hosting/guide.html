1. What is Grid Survival Simulator?

Grid Survival Simulator is an offline risk analysis tool for Forex & Gold traders who use Grid or Martingale strategies.

Before you risk real money, this app shows you:

  • How many grid levels your account can survive
  • When your account will hit stop-out
  • What's your worst-case drawdown
  • Where your basket breaks even
💡 100% Offline: Your data never leaves your device. No internet required. No login needed.

2. Getting Started

1 Open the app

You'll see the Quick Calculator screen. This is where you enter your strategy parameters.

2 Fill in your parameters

Enter your account balance, leverage, lot size, multiplier, and grid distance. See section 3 for details.

3 Tap Calculate

The app will show your Risk Dashboard with all key metrics.

4. Risk Dashboard

After tapping Calculate, you'll see the Dashboard with key metrics:

MetricWhat it means
Survivable LevelsHow many grid levels your account can handle before stop-out. E.g., "7/10" means 7 out of 10 levels are safe.
Max Drawdown %Worst-case drawdown if all levels trigger.
Stop-out PriceThe price at which your account gets margin-called.
Basket BreakevenThe price where your entire grid breaks even.
Rebind DistanceHow far price needs to move back to recover.
Total ExposureTotal lots across all triggered levels.
Constraint CheckPASS/FAIL for each risk constraint you set.
⚠️ Pre-flight Warning: If you see a red banner, your strategy violates one or more risk constraints. Adjust your parameters before going live!

Scroll down to see the Grid Levels Table showing each level's lot, entry price, cumulative lot, and margin required.

5. Price Ladder (Interactive Chart)

Tap "Price Ladder" on the Dashboard to see a visual chart:

  • Green line — Average entry price
  • Orange line — Basket breakeven price
  • Red dotted line — Stop-out price (if calculated)
  • Blue dots — Individual grid levels

Tap any level on the chart to see its details (lot size, entry price, margin).

6. What-If Slider

Tap "What-If" to simulate different price scenarios:

  • Drag the slider left or right to change the hypothetical price
  • See in real-time how many levels trigger at that price
  • Check your drawdown, margin level, and floating P/L
💡 Tip: Use this to answer "What happens if gold drops 500 pips?" or "What if EURUSD rallies 200 pips?"

7. Reverse Mode (Find Max Lot)

Instead of testing a fixed lot, let the app find the maximum safe lot size for you:

  1. Go to Reverse Mode from the Dashboard
  2. Enter your risk constraints (Max DD%, Min Margin Level%, Max Lot, Max Loss)
  3. Tap "Calculate"
  4. The app uses binary search to find the largest initial lot that satisfies ALL constraints
💡 Tip: The result shows which constraint is the "bottleneck" — the one limiting your lot size the most.

8. Save & Load Strategies

Save your strategy configurations for later:

  • Save: Tap the save icon on the Dashboard → enter a name → Done
  • Load: Go to "Saved Strategies" → tap any saved strategy
  • Delete: Long-press or tap the delete icon → confirm
📝 Note: You can save up to 5 strategies in the current version. All data is stored locally on your device.

9. Share as Image

Generate a summary card and share it:

  1. Tap "Share" on the Dashboard
  2. Review the generated image card
  3. Tap "Share" to send via WhatsApp, Telegram, Zalo, etc.

The image includes: Symbol, Direction, Lot, Multiplier, Levels, Distance, Survivable Levels, Max DD, Breakeven, Stop-out, and Exposure.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does this app trade for me?

No. This is a calculation/analysis tool only. It does not connect to any broker or execute trades.

Q: Is this financial advice?

No. This app is for educational and analytical purposes only. All trading decisions are your own responsibility.

Q: Why are my saved strategies gone?

Saved strategies are stored locally. If you uninstall the app, all data is lost. Make sure to save important strategies and share them as images for backup.

Q: Can I use this for crypto or stocks?

The app currently supports XAUUSD (Gold) and EURUSD. More instruments will be added in future updates.

Q: The numbers don't match my MT4/MT5. Why?

Different brokers have different:

  • Spread values
  • Commission structures
  • Swap rates
  • Stop-out level calculations

Use the Advanced Settings to input your broker's exact values for more accurate results.

Q: Is my data safe?

Yes. All data stays on your device. The app works 100% offline. No data is sent to any server.
